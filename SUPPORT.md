# Curlr Support

Curlr is currently in beta. The fastest route depends on what you need:

- **Downloads:** https://curlr.dev/downloads
- **Product overview:** https://curlr.dev
- **Why Curlr:** https://curlr.dev/why
- **Community support:** https://discord.gg/PCU5rVysw
- **General contact:** info@curlr.dev
- **Security reports:** security@curlr.dev

When requesting help, include your operating system, Curlr version, what you tried, and the exact error message after removing secrets.

Never publish API keys, passwords, access tokens, cookies, authorization headers, private URLs, customer data, or unredacted logs.
